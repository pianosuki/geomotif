# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good
faith and knows something you do not.

## The longer version

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. What that asks for, in practice:

- Take criticism of your code as criticism of your code.
- Give criticism of someone's code as criticism of their code.
- Be patient with people who are new to the maths, to Python, or to both.
  "Where do I even start" is a reasonable question and deserves a real answer.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — about anyone, in any
  venue connected to this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to pianosuki@protonmail.com. Reports are handled
privately, and the person reporting will not be named without their agreement.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not meet this
standard, and may ban repeat offenders. Enforcement follows the Contributor
Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
